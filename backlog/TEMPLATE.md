# F### / B### — Short Title

**Status:** Todo
**Type:** Feature | Bug
**Created:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Description

What the item is.
- For features: describe the capability, user flow, or behavior.
- For bugs: describe broken behavior (expected vs. actual, steps to reproduce, affected platforms/versions).

## Why It's Needed

The user or product reason. What problem does this solve? Who feels the pain today? Tie to user research, support tickets, or strategic priorities when possible.

## Business Case

The value or impact justification. Why is this worth doing now vs. later? Frame in terms 23blocks cares about:
- Growth / activation / retention
- Support load reduction
- Strategic positioning vs. competitors
- Unblocking a customer or partner deal
- Risk mitigation (security, compliance, reliability)

## Implementation Plan

A brief technical outline — not a full design doc. Enough that another engineer can pick it up:
- Key files / modules touched
- New dependencies or API endpoints needed
- Rough effort estimate (S / M / L, or hours/days)
- Open questions or risks
- Suggested sprint cycle to slot it into (if known)
